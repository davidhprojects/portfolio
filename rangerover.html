2006 Range Rover HSE Restoration

Full mechanical and interior restoration of a luxury British SUV, featuring custom modifications and system rebuilds

Summer 2021 David Herrera Mechanical & Interior

Overview

This project was the restoration of a 2006 Range Rover HSE — a luxury V8-powered British SUV picked up on Facebook Marketplace for $1,000. The goal was to bring the vehicle back to life while learning the mechanical and electrical systems that define British automotive design.

Over the course of several months I worked through extensive repairs and a few custom upgrades across multiple systems — from the air suspension to the interior — and came away with a deep appreciation for how English engineering prioritizes luxury and comfort over maintainability.

The Work

  • Replaced air struts, compressor unit, and repaired leaking air lines; recalibrated height sensors
  • Full interior refresh — new leather seats, door panels, carpeting, refinished trim, restored seat electronics
  • Rebuilt the power steering system — new pump and high-pressure lines, eliminated leaks and steering noise
  • Replaced A/C compressor and refrigerant lines; vacuumed and recharged the system
  • Installed a custom fiber-optic starlight headliner wired into the factory harness (Rolls-Royce style)
  • Diagnosis, sourcing, and integration of parts across all major subsystems

The Economics

  • Purchase price: $1,000 (Facebook Marketplace)
  • Parts & repairs: ~$2,000
  • Sale price: $5,000
  • Net profit: ~$2,000

A nice return on what was essentially a summer’s worth of wrenching and problem-solving in the driveway.

Skills & Takeaways

  • Hands-on experience with air-suspension systems and ride-height calibration
  • Worked through interior trim and seat electronics on a luxury platform
  • Practiced A/C system service — evacuation, recharge, leak detection
  • Wiring and integration of custom electronics into a factory harness
  • Takeaway: English luxury vehicles are built for comfort, not for the home mechanic
