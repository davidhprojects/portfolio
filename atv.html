2007 Polaris Sportsman 500 EFI Restoration

A home restoration of a Facebook Marketplace ATV completed in high school

Summer 2020 David Herrera Full Restoration

Overview

Purchased from Facebook Marketplace for $1,500, this Polaris Sportsman 500 was restored to working condition. Over the course of a summer, the ATV was stripped down to the chassis, with many key systems overhauled.

Systems Overhauled

  • Engine & Cooling: Replaced fuel injector, water pump impeller, and radiator
  • Electrical: Replaced a litany of fuses, added additional headlights alongside replacing existing bulbs
  • Suspension: Replaced all bushings and tie rods
  • Brakes: Replaced all brake pads, calipers, and brake cylinder pumps
  • Seat: Added in missing foam, replaced seat cover
  • Fuel System: Replaced fuel lines, fuel pump, and pressure regulator

The Economics

  • Purchase price: $1,500
  • Sold for: $3,400 (same day it was listed!)

The proceeds of this project went to purchasing my college laptop, which I used to craft this website!

Skills & Takeaways

  • First major vehicle restoration — built confidence in tackling mechanical projects
  • Learned to diagnose and replace fuel system components
  • Gained experience with suspension, brakes, and electrical systems
  • Completed a full strip-down and reassembly of a powersport vehicle
