2009 Ford Expedition 5.4L 3V Engine Rebuild

Complete engine rebuild of a Ford V8 — rebuilt from the ground up over a summer with my father

Summer 2020 David Herrera Engine Rebuild

Overview

Over the course of a summer, my father and I rebuilt the 5.4L 3V V8 engine in our 2009 Ford Expedition from the ground up. The engine had developed serious internal issues that required a complete teardown and rebuild.

The Work

  • Removed cylinder heads and inspected internals — found broken valves and cracks in one head
  • Purchased and installed a new replacement cylinder head
  • Replaced the complete timing chain system — new cam phasers, timing chains, and guides
  • Reassembled and tested the engine

Skills & Takeaways

  • First experience with a complete V8 engine rebuild
  • Learned the fundamentals of Ford's 3-valve timing system and cam phaser operation
  • Gained hands-on experience diagnosing cylinder head failures
  • Worked closely with my dad — a great father-son project
