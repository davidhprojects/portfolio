2014 Ford F-150 Lariat Engine Swap

Complete engine swap and mechanical restoration of a modern Ford pickup

Summer 2025 David Herrera Engine Swap

Overview

In June 2025, I purchased this 2014 Ford F-150 Lariat from Facebook Marketplace for $4,000. The listing mentioned engine trouble, but the truck's clean body and well-kept interior made it a worthwhile project. After towing it home with another Ford Expedition, I began diagnosing the issue and found the original engine had overheated beyond repair.

A replacement engine was sourced from a local junkyard, and I completed a full engine swap along with several smaller repairs and upgrades. The project gave me valuable experience working on Ford's modern powertrain systems, wiring harnesses, and cooling components.

The Work

  • Diagnosed overheated 3.5L Twin Turbo engine — confirmed beyond repair
  • Removed old engine using hoist setup and cleaned engine bay
  • Sourced replacement engine from a local junkyard ($3,500)
  • Swapped broken components from the old engine with working ones
  • Installed replacement engine, reconnected wiring harnesses, hoses, and cooling system
  • Tested and verified proper operation

The Economics

  • Purchase price: $4,000
  • Replacement engine: $3,500
  • Parts & repairs: ~$1,500
  • Total investment: ~$9,000

The truck is still with us today and currently worth around $12,000 — a rewarding turnaround for a summer's worth of work and problem-solving.

Skills & Takeaways

  • Gained hands-on experience with Ford's modern powertrain systems
  • Learned to work with complex wiring harnesses and cooling components
  • Practiced engine removal and installation using a hoist
  • Developed diagnostic skills for identifying engine failure modes
