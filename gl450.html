2014 Mercedes GL450 Engine Overhaul

Complete engine overhaul and restoration of a luxury Mercedes-Benz SUV

Summer 2025 David Herrera Engine Overhaul

Overview

I purchased this Mercedes GL450 in the summer of 2025 as my passion project before starting my master's degree. The truck was in great cosmetic condition, but the motor sounded like a rattle can and needed serious work.

Main Issues

  • Timing sprockets were worn, causing rough engine operation
  • Camshaft was damaged as a result of the worn timing components

Other Work

  • Replaced missing catalytic converter
  • Replaced all belt pulleys and the belt
  • Replaced turbo coolant hoses
  • Installed Apple CarPlay display
  • Full maintenance — transmission oil change, engine oil, and various other knick-knacks

The Economics

  • Purchase price: $5,000 (including legal fees and taxes)
  • Parts: ~$2,000
  • Maintenance: ~$500
  • Total investment: ~$7,500

The car is now in exceptional condition — very comfortable, and it treats me well.

Skills & Takeaways

Besides turning many, many wrenches, the biggest takeaway with this one is to not give up. This car proved to be a nuisance to take apart and figure out — Mercedes are no joke. But persevering and figuring it out was incredibly rewarding.
